S-MU2000 Web

The MU2000 tone generator in your browser, running the genuine firmware. Everything is stored locally: ROMs persist in your browser (IndexedDB) after the first pick, and nothing is ever uploaded anywhere. Serve this directory over HTTP (for example npx -y serve dist) — file:// cannot load the wasm module.

Offline render

Upload ROMs + a MIDI file, render to WAV, play or download.

Live playback

Boot the synth, play it from WebMIDI inputs in realtime.
